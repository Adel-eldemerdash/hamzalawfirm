## Group 3 — Record volume

*[internal] The single most consequential question in the tool for fee determination.*

**Q3.1** In total, how many **personal data records relating to individuals** do you hold? *(An approximate estimate is sufficient — include employees, customers, contacts, marketing lists, and archives)*
- 1 to 100,000
- 100,000 to 500,000
- 500,000 to 1,000,000
- 1,000,000 to 2,000,000
- 2,000,000 to 3,000,000
- 3,000,000 to 4,000,000
- 4,000,000 to 5,000,000
- More than 5,000,000
- Not known

> The first band — up to 100,000 records — is **exempt from license fees** under Article 19 of the Executive Regulations. Exemption from the fee is not exemption from the license, nor from any other obligation.

**Q3.2** Within that first band, how many records? *(Shown only if Q4.1 = Temporary and Q3.1 = 1 to 100,000)*
- 1 to 25,000
- More than 25,000 up to 100,000
- Not known

> Why we ask: the license schedule (ER Article 19) is fee-exempt up to **100,000** records, while the permit schedule (ER Article 20) is fee-exempt only up to **25,000**. An organization between the two figures pays nothing under a license but does pay under a permit.
